Road Texture Agent 2026.10.12-placement4
Focused patch on top of the Saudi Decal Fit Update (saudi-fit3).

INSTALL
Stop the Python server. Back up the matching code files, then extract this
ZIP into the road-texture-agent folder containing server.py. Merge app/ and
ui/ without deleting either folder. Restart, Ctrl+F5, and confirm the console
version is 2026.10.12-placement4.

USE
Road markings now has an explicit Minimum road width for markings setting
(default 6 m). This is distinct from Highway / From width (default 18 m),
which classifies a highway. Roads below the marking cutoff receive neither
lane lines nor procedural junction decals. Imported artwork remains manually
controlled and is not restricted by this road-marking cutoff.

Press Update 2D texture and then Update view on an existing loaded result.
If the page was refreshed and lost its result, reload the alpha and generate
once. The new engine version also triggers the marking-only correction even
if the settings snapshot did not otherwise change.

CHANGES
- Crossings and stop/give-way lines reserve their entire approach apron.
  Lane markings no longer paint through that area, even when it is inside a
  continuous highway chain instead of at a segmented street endpoint.
- The same reservations clip 3D marking strips. Dashed paint is omitted as a
  whole dash rather than sliced through a pedestrian crossing; continuous
  lines stop at the apron. Raster antialiasing caps also stay outside it.
- Reservations follow output resolution changes and are recorded with the
  junction marking data. Moving/removing decals recalculates the reserved
  regions, restoring the lane paint that is no longer blocked.
- Highway replacement now requires matching centreline position and road
  direction. A crossing or parallel neighboring road is not silently stripped
  of its own markings merely for lying inside a highway's wide envelope.
- 2D lane paint, 3D lane strips and procedural decal placement share the same
  minimum road width in metres. A manual lane split cannot bypass the cutoff.
- Materials, wear, street geometry, crossings' junction selection logic and
  highway group selections are retained. Explicit no-mark regions and the
  user's Markings area still take priority; a width cutoff is eligibility,
  not an instruction to paint over those exclusions or over junction cores.

CHECKS
Internal-road crossing exclusion: 2D dashed/solid paint and 3D strips.
Width cutoff at 5.9 m versus 6 m and user-set 12 m; both 2D and 3D.
Neighbor/crossing roads beside highways retain their own lane generation.
Procedural crossings below cutoff omitted; reservation resolution scaling.
Real 2048 x 1536 alpha: lane/decal generation completes; four eligible nearby
segments that the proximity test previously suppressed are retained.
Saudi fitting, original crossing crash, preset controls, syntax checks,
settings/history, material preservation and partial 3D update checks pass.
The screenshots' exact live generation was not available locally, and full

Only program files are included. No workspace, training, materials, uploads,
generated outputs or the Saudi PDF are replaced.
